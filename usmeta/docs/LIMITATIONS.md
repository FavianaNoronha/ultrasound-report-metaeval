# LIMITATIONS (read before quoting any number from this project)

This file is linked from every `results.md` in `results/partA/`. Dates and numbers refer to the results folders listed in `results/partA/INDEX.md`.

1. **Reference reports are templated.** They are built from dataset labels and masks by our own templates (`usmeta/templates_v2.py`): synthetic but fact-correct. There is no real clinician-written ultrasound report text in any of the 10 datasets. Real clinician text exists only in HiSBreast (not built yet; Vietnamese, to be translated). Conclusions about real reports are not supported by Part A.
2. **Layer 1 (`finding_f1`) and Layer 3 (`mask_verified_facts`, `grounded_check` on true masks) are upper bounds under templated text.** Their extractor or checker uses the same facts, wording and masks that built the reference, so AUROC 1.00 on templated text is close to guaranteed (rule extractor agrees with the labels on 99.99% of 126,840 fields). It is not evidence that these scorers work on real or model-written reports. The true-mask check cannot fail on true text (100% of position/size claims backed); the real test needs predicted masks (Part B).
3. **The AI judge is prompt-sensitive and its prompts were chosen on dev data.** Changing the wording of the instructions moved AUROC against harmless_small by up to 0.30 on 20 cases per dataset (BUS-BRA 0.91 -> 0.61, BUSI 0.99 -> 0.66, UDIAT 0.68 -> 0.50; Step 7 vs rerun2). Prompt_v2 was chosen because identical copies must score 1.0 (100 of 100), not because of its AUROC. Final numbers come from the locked test split (Step 10d). One judge model (Gemma-2-9B, 4-bit) and small samples; the original RadFact and a Claude judge were not run.
4. **Reruns and retractions (all kept).** (a) Step 4: the headline "Layer 0 scorers are below chance" is retracted as mainly an edit-size artefact: our harmless rewordings changed 5-10 times more text than a planted error. (b) Step 5 rerun2 added a matched one-word synonym group, after which BLEU/ROUGE-L rise above chance while chrF stays below it and BERTScore sits at chance. (c) Step 5 rerun3: BUS-BRA `Side = single` (253 images) had been written as "single breast"; fixed. (d) Step 7 rerun2: stricter judge prompt. (e) Step 6: the LLM extractor first scored 82.9% (below the 85% stop rule) before normalisation and a prompt fix; validated on a fresh sample (98.5%).
5. **Report-writing models and possible exposure.** Qwen2.5-VL, MedGemma and LLaVA-Med may have seen BUSI, BUS-BRA, TN3K, TN5000, HC18 (and others) during training; this is unverifiable (see `contamination_notes.md` in Step 10f). UltraG thyroid text (used in the earliest stage-1 benchmark) is GPT-5.5-written from masks, not radiologist text. Pretrained scorers (BERTScore, RaTEScore, Gemma) cannot be decontaminated by us; we only control our own data and splits.
6. **Severity labels are provisional.** They are guideline-based guesses (BI-RADS 3 -> 4a crosses the biopsy line, side swap, size error bands, a fetal head-circumference ladder with no source) pending clinician ranking. Severity Spearman and critical-only AUROC depend on them.
7. **Template sources marked "to confirm" are not verified.** The BI-RADS, TI-RADS and ISUOG structure in `docs/templates_sources.md` was written from memory. DDTI uses 4a/4b/4c TI-RADS levels, which differ from ACR TR1-TR5.
8. **A judge never grades reports from its own model family.** Part A only judges templated text with Gemma-2-9B. A Gemma judge must not grade MedGemma reports and a Qwen extractor must not grade Qwen reports.
9. **Datasets and splits.** Images are not in git; licences are "to confirm" for all 10 datasets. HC18 and the "Diverse Fetal Head Images" folder are the same images (resized); no benchmark dataset shares a near-duplicate with another. Within-dataset near-duplicates exist (BUSI 37% of images, FetalHead 9%, BUS-UCLM 6.6%, TN5000 5.6%) and are grouped, not dropped. BUSI, UDIAT, TN3K, TN5000 and HC18 have no patient/exam id, so the same patient may appear in more than one split ("group = image, patient overlap possible"). TN3K/TN5000 class 0/1 has no confirmed meaning and is left out of the reports. BUS-BRA `side` is not mask-checkable. Orientation and position are proxies (bounding box, image thirds), not anatomy. BrEaST size is approximate (pixel-size unit inferred).
10. **Not run:** GREEN (7B model too large for 16 GB), Claude judge (no credit), Jev (paid API, needs approval), original RadFact, SAM/MedSAM predicted masks, MedGemma and LLaVA-Med reports, clinician review.

11. **HiSBreast translations validated by automatic key-fact checks and independent back-translation; no bilingual human review.** The Vietnamese keyword dictionary used by the checks was written by the assistant; only 101 of 427 breast exams (24%) passed all three checks, so the kept set is a small, easier-than-average subset; the text and translations are kept local (blank licence). See `results/partA/step15_hisbreast/`.

We found no ultrasound work that tests the scorers with planted errors, severity and harmless rewordings; our search was not exhaustive. Nothing here is a claim of being first.
