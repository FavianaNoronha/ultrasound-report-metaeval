# LIMITATIONS (read before quoting any number from this project)

This file is linked from every `results.md` in `results/partA/`. Dates and numbers refer to the results folders listed in `results/partA/INDEX.md`.

1. **Reference reports are templated.** They are built from dataset labels and masks by our own templates (`usmeta/templates_v2.py`): synthetic but fact-correct. There is no real clinician-written ultrasound report text in any of the 10 datasets. Real clinician text exists only in HiSBreast (not built yet; Vietnamese, to be translated). Conclusions about real reports are not supported by Part A.
2. **Layer 1 (`finding_f1`) and Layer 3 (`mask_verified_facts`, `grounded_check` on true masks) are upper bounds under templated text.** Their extractor or checker uses the same facts, wording and masks that built the reference, so AUROC 1.00 on templated text is close to guaranteed (rule extractor agrees with the labels on 99.99% of 126,840 fields). It is not evidence that these scorers work on real or model-written reports. The true-mask check cannot fail on true text (100% of position/size claims backed); the real test needs predicted masks (Part B).
3. **The AI judge is prompt-sensitive and its prompts were chosen on dev data.** Changing the wording of the instructions moved AUROC against harmless_small by up to 0.30 on 20 cases per dataset (BUS-BRA 0.91 -> 0.61, BUSI 0.99 -> 0.66, UDIAT 0.68 -> 0.50; Step 7 vs rerun2). Prompt_v2 was chosen because identical copies must score 1.0 (100 of 100), not because of its AUROC. Final numbers come from the locked test split (Step 10d). One judge model (Gemma-2-9B, 4-bit) and small samples; the original RadFact and a Claude judge were not run.
4. **Reruns and retractions (all kept).** (a) Step 4: the headline "Layer 0 scorers are below chance" is retracted as mainly an edit-size artefact: our harmless rewordings changed 5-10 times more text than a planted error. (b) Step 5 rerun2 added a matched one-word synonym group, after which BLEU/ROUGE-L rise above chance while chrF stays below it and BERTScore sits at chance. (c) Step 5 rerun3: BUS-BRA `Side = single` (253 images) had been written as "single breast"; fixed. (d) Step 7 rerun2: stricter judge prompt. (e) Step 6: the LLM extractor first scored 82.9% (below the 85% stop rule) before normalisation and a prompt fix; validated on a fresh sample (98.5%).
5. **Report-writing models and possible exposure.** Qwen2.5-VL, MedGemma and LLaVA-Med may have seen BUSI, BUS-BRA, TN3K, TN5000, HC18 (and others) during training; this is unverifiable (see `contamination_notes.md` in Step 10f). UltraG thyroid text (used in the earliest stage-1 benchmark) is GPT-5.5-written from masks, not radiologist text. Pretrained scorers (BERTScore, RaTEScore, Gemma) cannot be decontaminated by us; we only control our own data and splits.
6. **Severity labels are provisional.** They are guideline-based guesses (BI-RADS 3 -> 4a crosses the biopsy line, side swap, size error bands, a fetal head-circumference ladder with no source) pending clinician ranking. Severity Spearman and critical-only AUROC depend on them.
7. **Template sources marked "to confirm" are not verified.** The BI-RADS, TI-RADS and ISUOG structure in `docs/templates_sources.md` was written from memory. DDTI uses 4a/4b/4c TI-RADS levels, which differ from ACR TR1-TR5.
8. **A judge never grades reports from its own model family.** Part A only judges templated text with Gemma-2-9B. A Gemma judge must not grade MedGemma reports and a Qwen extractor must not grade Qwen reports.
9. **Datasets and splits.** Images are not in git; licences are "to confirm" for all 10 datasets. HC18 and the "Diverse Fetal Head Images" folder are the same images (resized); no benchmark dataset shares a near-duplicate with another. Within-dataset near-duplicates exist (BUSI 37% of images, FetalHead 9%, BUS-UCLM 6.6%, TN5000 5.6%) and are grouped, not dropped. BUSI, UDIAT, TN3K, TN5000 and HC18 have no patient/exam id, so the same patient may appear in more than one split ("group = image, patient overlap possible"). TN3K/TN5000 class 0/1 has no confirmed meaning and is left out of the reports. BUS-BRA `side` is not mask-checkable. Orientation and position are proxies (bounding box, image thirds), not anatomy. BrEaST size is approximate (pixel-size unit inferred).
10. **Not run:** GREEN (7B model too large for 16 GB), Claude judge (no credit), Jev (paid API, needs approval), original RadFact, LLaVA-Med reports, clinician review. (MedGemma reports: Step 14. MedSAM predicted masks: item 12.)

11. **HiSBreast translations validated by automatic key-fact checks and independent back-translation; no bilingual human review.** The Vietnamese keyword dictionary used by the checks was written by the assistant; only 101 of 427 breast exams (24%) passed all three checks, so the kept set is a small, easier-than-average subset; the text and translations are kept local (blank licence). See `results/partA/step15_hisbreast/`.

12. **Part B (predicted masks) is small and optimistic.** MedSAM was prompted with boxes taken from the TRUE masks (so it is not asked to find the lesion), on 30 test scans per dataset (18 for BUS-UCLM); at most 3 scans per group; possible training exposure of MedSAM to the public datasets, unverifiable. The predicted-mask check works on breast/thyroid planted errors (AUROC 0.91 pooled) but fails on fetal head (skull ring vs interior) and gives correct reports a mean of 0.68. See `results/partA/step16_partB/`.

13. **Step 18 (threshold crossing) uses SYNTHETIC sizes.** No dataset has both a size in mm and a TI-RADS level, so the nodule size in mm is synthetic (real DDTI TI-RADS level; 4a/4b/4c mapped to ACR TR4, "to confirm"; thresholds "to confirm against Tessler 2017"; our size stands in for the maximum dimension). DDTI only, TR4 dominates (TR3 and TR5 too small), four sizes per real case are not independent, controls sit at larger sizes than the pair cases. Result is exploratory and says nothing about where real nodule sizes lie. The H3 check is on BrEaST at 20 mm ("to confirm", not a BI-RADS rule) with boxes from the true masks. See `results/partA/step18_threshold_crossing/`.

14. **Steps 19a–19c and 20.** Paired-preference numbers reuse saved scores; the judge cut-off is pooled over val datasets (no per-dataset val judge scores). The box sweep (19b) degrades prompts that are still derived from the true masks: it is not a detector. The tolerance-aware check (Step 20) hides small size errors by design. The manifest table (19c) lists all licences, URLs and citations as "to confirm".

15. **Step 21 robustness tests.** (a) Label-free prompts (centre, whole image, random, wrong region) are not a detector; random and wrong-region boxes reuse the true box SIZE (a leak), and "no lesion" claims are caught by any non-empty mask. (b) The dose-response judge cells use a capped sample (about 12 cases per dataset plus reused Step 12 replies; fetal head 27 cases); the image-check cells cover only the Step 16 scans (thyroid 36, fetal head 18 cases per cell). (c) The judge-sensitivity sample is 10 cases per dataset (89 cases; per-dataset cells too small to compare), with two hand-written paraphrases that were not searched. (d) The human-rewording sheet is prepared but NOT scored: no result about human paraphrases exists yet.

16. **Steps 21f and 22.** The split audit (21f) is a count of dHash clusters, not a pixel-level or patient-level proof: at 4 bits there are no clusters across splits, at a looser 8 bits there are 164 (not verified as duplicates). Step 22 tables reuse saved results, so they inherit every limit above; the failure taxonomy mixes samples of different sizes (see the file paths). A full-history text search for HiSBreast strings did not finish; only a restricted search (HiSBreast-named files, Step 11a/15 folders, docs, all .md/.csv) was completed and found nothing. Dataset-derived label files (`baseline_exp/breast.xlsx`, `tn3k_records.json`) are in git history and cannot be removed without rewriting it; their licences are "to confirm". Step 21e was not run.

We found no ultrasound work that tests the scorers with planted errors, severity and harmless rewordings; our search was not exhaustive. Nothing here is a claim of being first.

17. **Step 24 box reliability.** Every perturbed box is derived from the TRUE mask, so the curve shows sensitivity of the image check to box quality, not detector performance. Box IoU is a poor single axis for position claims: shrink, enlarge and jitter keep the box centre near the lesion and position AUROC stays at 0.95 or higher down to box IoU about 0.09, while the label-free boxes of Step 21a (centre not tied to the lesion) give 0.31-0.54 at similar IoU, so the IoU thresholds read from the pooled bins mix families. Only the shift family moves the centre, and its largest tested shift (0.75 box widths) still gives 0.80, so the range tested never reaches the label-free regime. Size claims are judged against the box scale (mechanical) and against MedSAM, which undersizes by about 11-16% even with the exact box; size AUROC is highest for slightly enlarged boxes for that reason. Only the levels listed in the brief were run; the IoU-bin table mixes families; TN5000 "masks" are boxes.

18. **Step 21e matched edit-size.** Edit size is set by the error type (measurement and category errors are 0-10%, extra-lesion additions 10-20%, the "No focal lesion is seen." rewrite 40%+), so an edit-size bin is also an error-type bin and the bins cannot separate the two effects. No error falls in the 20-40% bin and large harmless rewordings only populate 20%+. Judge rows use the capped judged subset (5-22 groups in several bins). Human rewordings (29) are almost all large edits.

19. **Step 21g leakage sensitivity.** The leak list comes from <= 8-bit dHash clusters inside one dataset (loose, not verified duplicates; not checked across datasets or at patient level beyond the group ids). Breast has 8 candidate cases to drop (0 image-check scans), so the breast result says almost nothing about leakage robustness; thyroid and fetal head have more. Dropping images changes the test mix (not only leakage), so the change is a sensitivity measure, not proof of no leakage.

20. **Step 25 error coverage.** Omission candidates are synthetic (one sentence dropped from a templated reference), several dropped sentences are labels (pathology / assessment) rather than imaging findings, and thyroid / fetal-head references are very short, so a dropped sentence is a large relative change. The saved judge was not run on them. The mapping of planted types to the six groups is our own (count and position go to `other`; "No focal lesion is seen." and flipped halo / calcification / foci to negation). Negation looks easy for word scorers only because it is the whole-report rewrite (Step 21e).

21. **Step 27 `llm_finding_f1`.** The benchmark text is templated and the few-shot examples are templated, so 0.99-1.00 measures how well the extractor reads our own wording and rewordings, not free prose; it is not better than the regex `finding_f1` (1.00). "Known facts" for the extractor check are the rule extractor's reading of the same templated text. Judge columns use the 59-case judged subset. The stability comparison (dev pairs vs the judge's test sample) is indicative, not like for like. The extractor drops a fact when the wording changes ("Cranial circumference": 13 fetal-head candidates), reads size digits wrongly in rare cases and inferred `pathology: normal` once; on the 29 human rewordings it reads 76% to the same facts as the reference (too few to compare). One extractor model, one prompt, greedy, one machine.

22. **Step 28 natural errors.** 60 images per organ (not 100), one small writer (Qwen2.5-VL-3B) and one prompt. Fetal head has no checkable report (the writer states no head circumference or plane) and thyroid only 37 checkable reports with 5 wrong, so the wrong-vs-correct result rests on breast (35 wrong, 25 correct). Truth is the label facts, accuracy is read by an LLM extractor (shared with `llm_finding_f1`), and the 30-report hand check is still to be filled in. Key facts are only those the labels have (thyroid size is a % of image width that the writer never gives). The frozen judge's `judge_score` is floored at 0 on long free-text reports, so only `sentence_f1` carries judge information here. Word scores on long markdown against one-sentence references are dominated by length.

23. **Step 28 extractor hand check.** On 30 hand-checked free-text reports the Qwen7B extractor was right in 22 (all 12 fetal-head ones only because they are empty) and in 10 of 18 breast and thyroid reports (56%, 34-75%); its errors are mostly invented or over-read fields (presence, count, composition, pathology, halo, margin). The Step 28 "wrong key fact" labels and fact F1 inherit this noise, and `llm_finding_f1` shares it; the sample is 30 reports from one writer, so the rate itself is uncertain. This supersedes the "hand check pending" wording in item 22.

24. **Step 29 segmentation.** The U-Net is trained and tested on the same datasets (different groups; BUSI, UDIAT, TN3K, TN5000 and HC18 have no patient ids, so patient overlap cannot be excluded; only dev images in <= 8-bit clusters reaching val/test were dropped). Random-init encoder, one seed, one split, no repeat. The image check runs on the 228 scans of Steps 21a/24 and on our templated candidates; thyroid position (0.80, interval 0.69-0.89) is on the decision boundary. TN5000 has boxes only (no Dice; 30 scans, the only cross-dataset check). For head circumference the tolerance-aware rule tuned on breast/thyroid size (tau 0.3) is unusable (0.52 even with the labelled HC); the exact rule is blind to +-1 mm errors at a 2.7 mm MAE; oracle-geometry rows are circular. FetalHead has no pixel size and was not tested. The planted-HC check was rescored once from saved predictions after a bug fix (no second test read).

25. **Step 30 severity.** Severity levels (1-3) are our own provisional, rule-assigned labels and have never been confirmed by a clinician. They are confounded with error type (level 3 is mostly lesion presence, pathology, side and category jumps) and with edit size (the whole-report "no lesion" rewrite is level 3), so a per-level AUROC cannot separate "how severe" from "what kind" or "how many words changed". Judge cells use the judged subset (several fetal-head cells have fewer than 30 groups); image-check rows cover only mask-checkable fields on the 228 breast and thyroid scans (fetal head not covered).

26. **Files in git history that may not be redistributable (history not rewritten).** Dataset licences are all "to confirm". `baseline_exp/breast.xlsx` (BrEaST clinical / label spreadsheet) and `baseline_exp/tn3k_records.json` (TN3K labels) were committed in the first commit and are derived from those datasets. Benchmark, report and judge files built from dataset labels or images (`usmeta/bench*.jsonl`, `true_reports_v2*.jsonl`, `reports_qwen*.jsonl`, `reports_medgemma*.jsonl`, `judge_*.jsonl`, `memprobe_*.jsonl`, `duplicates.jsonl`, `splits.jsonl`) carry dataset case ids and label content (templated or model-written text and image hashes). `reference to human.xlsx` holds the owner's rewordings of our own templated text. No images, model weights, HiSBreast text, translation or exam id are tracked (checked 2026-11-11 with `git grep` and a scan for Vietnamese text: only code, keyword dictionaries, exam-type names and synthetic test strings). Treat the dataset-derived files as possibly not redistributable if the repository is made public; see also `LICENSE-NOTES.md` and `ROADMAP.md`.

27. **Step 33 edit-size law.** One regression per scorer on 9-15 (organ x harmless group) cells that share references and error candidates; the bootstrap resamples groups, not cells, so the interval does not capture the choice of groups or the organ effect. The judge and `llm_finding_f1` use the judged subset (59 cases per dataset) and have fewer cells (no "other" group; no per-pair judge scores for the human rewordings), so their R² values are not comparable cell for cell with the word scorers'. Fetal-head cells have very short texts, so their edit ratios and AUROCs are extreme (the fit is also given without cells under 30 groups). R² >= 0.6 means edit size predicts the AUROC, not that a scorer is right.

28. **Step 31 cross-dataset segmenter.** Breast only (thyroid and fetal head not run), one seed, one split per fold, random-init encoder; per-dataset cells have 30 scans or fewer (18 for BUS-UCLM) and are too small to compare, only the pooled row has 30 or more groups. "Held out" removes a dataset, not similar scanners or re-used images in the other datasets (no cross-dataset duplicates were found at <= 4 bits, Step 10a; looser similarity was not checked across datasets). The image check is scored on our templated candidates and on the 228-scan selection of Steps 21a / 24 (breast part).

29. **Step 32 extractor v3.** All free-text results of Step 32 are provisional: v3 has not been hand-checked (`docs/step32_check.csv` is unfilled). The replay of the owner's v2 notes shows what v3 removes, not whether the rest is right, and 7 of the 10 extractions marked right for v2 change under v3 (possible lost facts). The Step 28 labels and `llm_finding_f1` depend on the extractor, so v2 and v3 AUROCs are not independent; the thyroid presence mapping (nothing stated means "absent") is unchanged from Step 27; same 180 reports, one writer, one prompt change, greedy decoding.

30. **Step 33 factorial.** Templated text: cell (d) is the same error in the terse or long template (checked with the regex extractor that reads the same template); real paraphrases of an erroneous report are not tested. Blocks share references and errors, so the bootstrap resamples reference groups; eta-squared shares include the spread across errors and references in the residual. The judge rows rest on 150 new calls (239 blocks, 71 groups; fewer than the 12-cases-per-dataset cap allows because few judged cases have all four cells judged); per-organ judge cells are around 30 groups. 216 histology error rows cannot be regenerated by the frozen builder and are excluded. The brief's reversal rate (d below a) is about 1.00 for every scorer and does not separate fact from wording; the clinically relevant reversal (c above b) is reported next to it, as pre-registered.

31. **Step 35 limit of detection.** AUROC is measured against matched one-word synonyms on templated text, so a scorer's value follows edit size as well as the error (Steps 21e, 33); levels pool both directions (the image check is asymmetric) and small sizes round to integers, so small levels have fewer cases. Judge rows use a capped subset (12 cases per dataset for the new candidates; many judge cells, notably fetal head, have fewer than 30 groups). Head circumference uses additive shifts only; the image checks for fetal head are Step 16 oracle-box masks (no U-Net). `llm_finding_f1` was not run. CIs of the limit are percentile intervals of the same rule over resamples and are coarse on a seven-level grid.

32. **Step 34 metamorphic checks.** Relations are rule-based and crude on free text (R5 deletes the first "no " or " not ", R4 changes a number), so some transformed texts are unnatural; R4 and R5 compare the error with a harmless synonym swap, which makes them a label-free version of the discrimination test, not an independent check of facts. delta = 0.05 on the 0-1 scale is a fixed choice (0.10 shown). The HiSBreast set is 41 machine-translated conclusions with few measurements (R1, R4, R6 apply to 0), so HiSBreast cells are too small to compare; its texts stay local and only aggregated rates are written. The judge and llm_finding_f1 ran on a capped subset (24 templated, 10 HiSBreast, 20 writer items, most cells under 30 applicable items; 24 rather than 20 templated because a case id picked for the writer subset also flags the same case's templated reference). Writer reports are scored against the label reference, long markdown, near-zero word-overlap scores.

33. **Step 36 adversarial attack.** Only wrong reports that the judge already penalises (saved score <= 0.7) were attacked, so errors the judge misses entirely are not covered; up to 10 per dataset (BUS-UCLM 6 on dev / val); five fixed, hand-written transforms on templated text, no search against the judge (an optimised or judge-in-the-loop attack was not tried). The PREREG's single "large harmless rewording" counterpart was operationalised as the better-scoring of the two style swaps. One judge model, one prompt, greedy; gains of a few hundredths are within the prompt noise of Step 21c. The test run used one transform chosen on dev / val.

34. **Step 37 RadGraph-F1 and GREEN.** RadGraph-F1 is a chest-X-ray entity and relation scorer (RadGraph-XL) applied to templated ultrasound text; its failure here (it ignores numbers, scores synonyms as entity changes) describes this use only. Only the 'partial' reward level was analysed (simple and complete are saved locally). Run on CPU in a separate venv with transformers pinned to 4.46.3 (the package breaks on version 5). The factorial verdict on the judge's 239-block subset (71 groups) is noisier than on all blocks. **GREEN has no result:** the official checkpoint could not score two pairs in 36 minutes on this machine (CPU float16, 16 GB RAM), so no GREEN-vs-judge statement can be made; a machine with a CUDA GPU or at least 32 GB would be needed. The 13 GB checkpoint and the two venvs sit outside the repository.
