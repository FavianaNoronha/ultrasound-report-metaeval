One row per dataset (10 benchmark datasets, the derived HC18 "Diverse" copy, HiSBreast). Full table: [`datasets/MANIFEST_TABLE.md`](../../../../datasets/MANIFEST_TABLE.md) (copy here: `MANIFEST_TABLE.md`). Candidate rows per image: `candidate_rows_per_image.json`.

**Headline:** no licence, URL or citation was verified (all "to confirm"); five datasets (BUSI, UDIAT, TN3K, TN5000, HC18) have no patient/exam id, so independent units are unknown there; candidates built from the same original are not independent (13–31 rows per image), so every interval resamples groups.

One row per dataset. **Nothing here was verified against the original source pages**: names, citations and years come from `datasets/manifest.json` ("written from memory, to confirm"), URLs and licences are **to confirm** unless stated, and no licence was guessed. Counts come from our own records (`usmeta/local_data`), `usmeta/splits.jsonl`, Step 10a (`results/partA/step10a_dedupe/`) and `bench_v2d.jsonl`. Images are not in this repository.

**Candidates built from the same original image are not independent.** Every original image (case) carries about 13–31 candidate rows in `bench_v2d` (one identical copy, harmless rewordings, and 4–20 single-error variants of the same reference text); within a case they share the same facts, and across cases near-duplicate images share near-identical reports. All intervals therefore resample **groups** (patient/exam where one exists, otherwise the image, with near-duplicate clusters merged into one group), never rows. Counting rows or candidates as independent samples would overstate precision.

## Reading notes
- **Independent units:** the unit of independence is the group, not the image and never the candidate row. "Unknown" means the dataset has no patient or exam id, so images of one patient may be in different splits (`docs/LIMITATIONS.md` item 9).
- **Reference text:** 10 of 11 text sources are our own templates over labels, masks or boxes (synthetic but fact-correct); only HiSBreast has clinician text.
- **Validation of labels:** this project did not check label quality independently; "not assessed here" does not mean the dataset papers did not.
- **Licences, URLs, citations:** all "to confirm" until a person reads each source page.
