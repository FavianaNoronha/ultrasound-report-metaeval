# Step 33 (2x2 factorial: fact x wording) pre-registration
Written and committed BEFORE any score of the new cell (d) exists and before any Step 33 test-split number is computed. The builder is checked on dev / val references only. Deviations after this commit are stated in `results.md` ("Deviations from the pre-registration").

## Design (breast and thyroid test references)
Per reference, four cells of candidate text:
- **(a) no error, small edit:** the existing harmless rewordings of type `small` (synonyms, reorder, unit) and `matched` (one-token synonym), pooled; the unit of analysis uses the mean score of a reference's (a) rewordings.
- **(b) no error, large edit:** the existing large harmless rewordings (style swaps `style_terse`, `style_long`).
- **(c) error, small edit:** the existing planted errors (one wrong fact, rendered in the standard style).
- **(d) error, large edit (NEW):** the same planted error on top of the large rewording: for each planted error (its modified facts, regenerated with the frozen `bench_v2.errors`, matched to the saved candidate by text) and each large style S in {terse, long}, `templates_v2.render(modified_facts, organ, S)` (the large-rewording generator applied to the erroneous report). **Check:** the regex extractor (`schema.extract_rule`) on the (d) text must give the planted new value for the changed field and not the old value, and the large-style harmless text of the same reference must not; otherwise the (d) text is dropped. The number built, dropped and the reasons are reported; if more than 30% of (d) candidates of an organ are dropped the result for that organ is labelled limited.
- Unit of analysis ("block") = (reference, planted error e, large style S) with four scores (a, b, c, d). Scorers: BLEU, ROUGE-L, chrF, BERTScore (roberta-large), RaTEScore, `finding_f1` (regex, upper bound) and the Gemma judge (`judge_score`, prompt_v2). Cells (a), (b), (c) use the saved test scores; only (d) is scored now (word / embedding scorers on all (d); the judge on at most 12 cases per dataset as in Step 21c, restricted to cases whose (a), (b), (c) were judged, and at most 3 planted errors per case, chosen with seed 0).

## Statistics and decision (fixed now)
- **Two-way decomposition** of the score over the four cells (fact: error vs none; wording: small vs large): eta-squared = sum of squares of the effect / total sum of squares, for fact, wording, interaction, and the residual share (within-cell spread across references and errors), over all blocks, per scorer; breast + thyroid pooled (primary) and per organ. 95% CI by bootstrap over reference groups (1,000 resamples, seed 0).
- **Decision (per scorer, pooled breast + thyroid): a scorer is "fact-driven" if eta-squared(fact) > eta-squared(wording); otherwise "wording-driven".** Per-organ results are reported next to it; where they disagree with the pooled verdict this is stated. Cells (organ x scorer) under 30 groups are marked too small to compare and do not change the pooled verdict.
- **AUROC at matched wording:** (c vs a) = P(score(c) < score(a)) and (d vs b) = P(score(d) < score(b)), per case (all c-a pairs / all d-b pairs of the case), ties 0.5, mean over cases, group bootstrap.
- **Reversal rate (as specified in the brief):** share of pairs (d, a) in which the error + large text scores **below** the correct + small text, over all pairs of a block's reference. Because a scorer driven by wording alone also gives d < a, this number does not separate fact from wording; **therefore the clinically relevant reversal is reported next to it:** the share of pairs (c, b) in which the **error + small** text scores **above** the **correct + large** text (an error outranking a harmless rewrite), and the share of (d, a) pairs in which d scores above a. Both are reported with group-bootstrap CIs.
- Test split read once through `usmeta.splits.load('test')` (logged); saved test scores are reused for (a), (b), (c).
